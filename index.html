About me

I got into programming out of pure excitement for building things, and that curiosity gradually pulled me toward data — I liked seeing how raw numbers could turn into decisions people actually act on. That interest led me toward working as a data analyst, and I've been building my skills in that direction ever since.

Day to day I work mainly in PostgreSQL and Power BI, using Python mostly for basic scripting and data cleaning with pandas, numpy, and matplotlib. One thing that's distinctive about how I work: I don't just clean a dataset and move on — I dig until I find the story in it, cross-checking numbers across multiple angles before trusting a conclusion, because a dashboard is only as good as the analyst's skepticism behind it.
